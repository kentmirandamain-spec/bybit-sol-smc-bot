# Adaptive timeframe backtest

Generated by `python backtest_adaptive.py`. This file is a research result. It does not turn the mode on.

- Source: **okx** public SOLUSDT 5-minute candles, resampled to 15m, 1h and 4h on clock buckets. Incomplete buckets are dropped.
- Test window: 2026-08-21 02:43 UTC → 2026-11-19 02:35 UTC (90 days), plus 20 days of warm-up that cannot fill a trade.
- Walk-forward split: first 60 days are in-sample (2026-08-21 02:43 UTC → 2026-10-20 02:43 UTC), last 30 days are out of sample (2026-10-20 02:43 UTC onward). Fills are counted by fill time.
- Fees: maker 0.0002 on the limit entry, taker 0.00055 on the stop or target, included in the 5 USDT risk the same way as `backtest.py`. **Slippage is not modelled.** Funding is not modelled.
- Caps on every variant, including the baseline: exactly 5 USDT risk, one open position, stop new entries after 3R (15 USDT) realized loss on that UTC day, at most 6 fills per UTC day, and a pause for the rest of the UTC day after 3 consecutive losses. Breakeven, trail, and partial take-profit are off so the timeframe choice is the only difference.
- The adaptive thresholds (ADX 18 / 22 / 30, ATR percentile 25 / 75 / 80 / 85, and the three pairs with R targets 2.0 / 1.5 / 2.5) were written down before this run. They were **not** searched on these 90 days. The row "fitted fixed pair" is the opposite: it is the fixed pair with the best in-sample expectancy and at least 8 in-sample trades (1h/5m), then frozen. That row exists to show selection bias. It is not the adaptive rule.
- Trades removed by the shared caps (closed signals → kept): 1h/15m baseline 36→36, 1h/5m 102→101, 4h/1h 12→12. A baseline that barely changes means this table is comparable to the earlier uncapped 1h/15m study.

Regime hours inside the test window (one label per closed 1h bar): strong_trend 249 hours, trend 478 hours, mixed 519 hours, range 522 hours, high_vol 392 hours, unknown 0 hours.

Adaptive trades by the regime that allowed them: mixed 24, strong_trend 3, trend 7.

## Out-of-sample verdict

adaptive did not beat the 1h/15m baseline out of sample: expectancy was not higher.

The in-sample picker chose **1h/5m** (73 trades, expectancy -0.179R). Out of sample that frozen pair was expectancy +0.168R over 28 trades (total +4.71R, drawdown 4.51R), against the baseline's +0.236R over 9 trades. The pair that looked best on the fit window was not the best out-of-sample expectancy.

The full-window adaptive result is not the decision. The rule can still be overfit: three pairs, three R targets, and the skip thresholds were chosen by hand from how this strategy already behaves, on the same SOL market the earlier 90-day studies used. A single 30-day holdout is one path, not a distribution. Slice drawdowns are measured inside that slice only. Out-of-sample max drawdown is not the drawdown from the in-sample equity peak.

**Recommendation: Do not install, including in dry run. Leave `adaptive_mode` false.**

`adaptive_mode` stays **false** in `config.yaml`. Default entries stay 1h bias and 15m. Dry run stays on. Live learning stays proposal-only. Nothing in this report places an order.
